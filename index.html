Research

Research overview

Service Design, Pricing, and Consumer Choice

I study how firms design services and make pricing and product-offering decisions in response to new technologies, consumer behavior, and social objectives. Using analytical models, I examine how these decisions shape consumer choices, firm profitability, and consumer welfare. My research focuses on human–AI service systems, socially responsible pricing, and the allocation of product visibility.

Human–AI Service Design

I study how firms redesign service systems when AI servers become available. My work examines how AI adoption changes service offerings, customer segmentation, and the roles of human and AI servers.

Featured paperRobot Adoption and Service Polarization

Socially Responsible Pricing

I study how firms incorporate social objectives into pricing decisions. This work examines the trade-off between profit and consumer surplus and how socially responsible pricing shapes market outcomes.

Featured paperSocially Responsible Pricing

Product Visibility and Consumer Choice

I study how firms coordinate product visibility with assortment and pricing. This work examines how limited attention and prominence affect consumer choice and the firm’s optimal decisions.

Featured paperCoordinating Spotlight Allocation with Assortment and Pricing
